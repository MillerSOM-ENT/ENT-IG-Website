Getting started with research access

Chart access, human-subjects training, and IRB registration — most projects above will ask for these, in roughly this order, before you can start.

EMR chart access

Epic/UChart (UMH) and Cerner (JMH). Requests process the first Friday of every month.

01

Download and fill in the UChart and Cerner access request forms.

02

Email both to your PI to sign.

03

Once signed, submit both through the same links above.

04

Watch your email — UChart assigns CBLs to complete before access is finalized.

CITI training

Required modules vary by research type — confirm with your PI.

01

Register an account at citiprogram.org.

02

Complete Good Clinical Practice (GCP), Human Research Subjects (Group 1: Biomedical Researchers), and Conflict of Interest (COI).

03

Save your completion certificates — eProst registration asks for them.

eProst / IRB access

Miami's IRB system — needed for anything requiring IRB approval.

01

On the research resources page, find "Office of the Vice Provost for Research & Scholarship."

02

Open "Institutional Review Boards (IRB)," then "Login to IBISResearch."

03

Log in or register using your UM credentials.

OHANCAW

Oral, Head & Neck Cancer Awareness Week.

Maintained by: OHANCAW Committee
Full event details for this year's OHANCAW week haven't been finalized yet — this section updates automatically the moment the OHANCAW Committee publishes them. No rebuild needed.

OHANCAW is a national awareness week focused on oral, head, and neck cancers — early detection, risk factors, and free or low-cost screening events. ENT IG typically participates with a screening event and educational programming; specifics for this year will post here as they're confirmed, closer to the event.
